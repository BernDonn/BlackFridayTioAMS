What is Predictive Analysis?

A business-friendly explanation

Predictive analysis uses historical data to estimate what is likely to happen next. In business, it helps you plan inventory, staffing, marketing budgets, and revenue targets.

In this dashboard
  • We merge sales and weather by date.
  • We fit a linear regression model: revenue depends on temperature.
  • We forecast Black Friday revenue and test “what-if” temperature scenarios.
Why scenarios?

Real life is uncertain. Scenarios help you explore how outcomes might change if conditions change. Here: if Black Friday is warmer or colder than average.

Note: This is a simplified educational model. In practice you would include more drivers (marketing, promotions, web traffic, competitor pricing) and validate accuracy more rigorously.
